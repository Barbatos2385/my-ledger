Backup and restore

Back up regularly. Your information is stored only in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, uninstalling the browser, or changing devices can erase it. CSV is for Excel; JSON is the full restorable backup including budgets and bills. No bank connection or cloud sync.

Privacy

This app stores data locally in your browser's IndexedDB. It has no analytics, accounts, or external data services. For sensitive financial information, use your phone's screen lock and keep exported backups in a secure location.
